Privacy Policy

Parlo – Learn English & French · Effective date: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Parlo is developed by Awais InfoTech (developer: Awais Ahmad), Lahore, Pakistan. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at lovingawais123@gmail.com.

The short version

Parlo does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Your lessons, dictionary searches, favorites, and progress never leave your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no registration, no login, and no server of ours that receives your information. We cannot see what you learn, search, or say in the app.

Speaking practice and the microphone

Parlo includes a speaking-practice feature: you say a phrase out loud and the app shows you how closely it matched. This feature uses the speech-recognition service built into your device (provided by your phone's operating system / Google), not a service of ours.

  • The microphone permission is used only when you tap the speak button in a lesson.
  • Your audio is processed by your device's speech-recognition service, which returns text to the app so it can check your answer.
  • Parlo itself does not record, save, store, or transmit your audio anywhere.
  • How the device speech service handles audio is governed by that provider's own privacy policy (for example, Google's).

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, a few things are stored only on your phone:

  • Lessons you have completed (your progress ticks).
  • Words and phrases you save to Favorites.

This information stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Offline use and the internet permission

All lessons, the 38,000-word English–French dictionary, quizzes, and favorites work fully offline. The app declares an internet permission only so your device's speech-recognition service can operate for the speaking-practice feature; Parlo itself does not upload your data.

Third parties

Parlo contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no third-party tracking. The only external component involved is your device's built-in speech-recognition and text-to-speech services, as described above.

Children's privacy

Because Parlo collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
